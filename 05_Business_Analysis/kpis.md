# KPI Analysis

## 1. KPI Overview

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) are measurable values used to evaluate business performance and support management decision-making.

For this e-commerce analysis, KPIs are grouped into the following areas:

- Revenue Performance
- Profitability
- Customer Performance
- Product Performance
- Operational Performance

---

## 2. Revenue KPIs

| KPI | Definition | Business Purpose | Primary Stakeholder |
|---|---|---|---|
| Total Revenue | Total sales revenue generated during the analysis period | Measures overall sales performance | Senior Management, Sales |
| Average Order Value (AOV) | Average revenue generated per order | Measures customer order value | Sales, Marketing |
| Revenue by Category | Revenue generated by each product category | Identifies major revenue contributors | Sales, Product Team |
| Revenue by Customer Segment | Revenue generated by different customer segments | Identifies valuable customer groups | Marketing, Sales |

---

## 3. Profitability KPIs

| KPI | Definition | Business Purpose | Primary Stakeholder |
|---|---|---|---|
| Total Profit | Total profit generated during the analysis period | Measures overall profitability | Senior Management, Finance |
| Profit Margin | Profit expressed as a percentage of revenue | Measures profitability relative to sales | Finance, Management |
| Profit by Product | Profit generated by individual products | Identifies profitable and underperforming products | Product Team, Finance |
| Profit by Category | Profit generated by product categories | Identifies category-level profitability | Product Team, Management |

---

## 4. Customer KPIs

| KPI | Definition | Business Purpose | Primary Stakeholder |
|---|---|---|---|
| Customer Count | Number of customers represented in the dataset | Measures customer base | Marketing, Management |
| Revenue by Age Group | Revenue generated by different customer age groups | Identifies high-value customer groups | Marketing |
| Revenue by Customer Segment | Revenue generated by each customer segment | Supports customer segmentation decisions | Marketing, Sales |
| Average Customer Value | Average revenue generated per customer | Helps understand customer contribution | Marketing, Management |

---

## 5. Product Performance KPIs

| KPI | Definition | Business Purpose | Primary Stakeholder |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sales by Product | Sales generated by each product | Identifies high and low-performing products | Sales, Product Team |
| Profit by Product | Profit generated by each product | Identifies product profitability | Finance, Product Team |
| Product Profit Margin | Profitability of individual products relative to revenue | Identifies products with profitability gaps | Product Team, Finance |
| Category Revenue | Revenue generated by each category | Supports category performance analysis | Product Team |

---

## 6. Operational KPIs

| KPI | Definition | Business Purpose | Primary Stakeholder |
|---|---|---|---|
| Order Count | Total number of orders | Measures transaction volume | Sales, Operations |
| Operational Performance Metrics | Available measures related to order or delivery performance | Identifies potential operational issues | Operations |
| Delivery Performance | Measures related to order delivery where available | Supports operational improvement | Operations, Management |

---

## 7. KPI-to-Business-Objective Mapping

| Business Objective | KPI | Business Question |
|---|---|---|
| Understand revenue performance | Total Revenue | How much revenue is the business generating? |
| Identify profitable areas | Total Profit, Profit Margin | Which areas contribute most to profitability? |
| Improve product performance | Product Revenue, Product Profit | Which products perform well or poorly? |
| Understand customer behavior | Customer Count, Revenue by Age Group | Which customer groups contribute most to revenue? |
| Improve category performance | Category Revenue, Category Profit | Which categories require attention? |
| Monitor operations | Order Count, Operational Metrics | Are there operational performance gaps? |
| Support management decisions | All relevant KPIs | What actions should management consider? |

## 8. KPI Usage in Power BI

The identified KPIs were incorporated into the Power BI analysis to provide management with a visual view of:

- Overall business performance
- Revenue performance
- Profitability
- Customer behavior
- Product performance
- Category performance
- Operational performance

The Power BI dashboard acts as the analytical layer, while the Business Analysis phase translates these measurements into business findings, requirements, and recommendations.

---

## 9. KPI Interpretation Approach

KPIs should not be interpreted independently.

For example:

### Revenue + Profit

A product with high revenue may not necessarily generate high profit.

Therefore:

> Revenue should be analyzed together with profit and profit margin.

### Customer Revenue + Customer Segment

A customer segment generating high revenue may require further analysis of:

- Profitability
- Order behavior
- Product preferences
- Customer value

### Product Revenue + Product Profitability

Products should be evaluated based on both:

- Sales contribution
- Profit contribution

This helps identify products that generate high sales but may have relatively lower profitability.

---

## 10. KPI Success Criteria

The KPI framework should enable stakeholders to:

1. Monitor overall business performance.
2. Identify revenue and profitability drivers.
3. Detect underperforming products or categories.
4. Understand important customer segments.
5. Identify potential operational issues.
6. Support data-driven decision-making.
7. Measure the impact of future business improvements.
