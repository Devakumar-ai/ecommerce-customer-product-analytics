# Gap Analysis

## 1. Purpose

Gap Analysis compares the current state of the e-commerce business with the desired future state.

The objective is to identify gaps in business performance, processes, monitoring, and decision-making and determine areas where improvement may be required.

---

## 2. Current State vs Desired State

| Business Area | Current State | Desired State | Identified Gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue Monitoring | Revenue is analyzed across products, categories, customers, and regions | Management has a consistent view of revenue drivers and trends | Need for structured revenue monitoring |
| Profitability | Profitability varies across products/categories and requires further investigation | Products and categories are evaluated using revenue, profit, and margin together | Profitability visibility gap |
| Product Performance | Product performance varies across categories | Management can identify high-performing and underperforming products | Product performance monitoring gap |
| Customer Analysis | Customer performance is analyzed by segments and age groups | High-value customer groups are clearly identified and monitored | Customer-value analysis gap |
| Returns | Fashion shows a higher return rate than the overall return rate | High-return categories/products are identified and investigated systematically | Return-management insight gap |
| Regional Performance | Regional revenue varies across locations | Regional differences are monitored and investigated | Regional performance monitoring gap |
| Discount Monitoring | Discounts are available as an analytical dimension | Discount impact on profitability is clearly monitored | Discount-profitability analysis gap |
| Operational Monitoring | Operational metrics are available for analysis | Operational KPIs are consistently monitored and linked to business outcomes | Operational KPI gap |
| Root Cause Analysis | Performance differences have been identified | Root causes are investigated using structured analysis | Root-cause investigation gap |
| Decision-Making | Power BI provides analytical insights | Insights are converted into documented actions and requirements | Insight-to-action gap |

---

## 3. Key Business Gaps

### Gap 1 — Profitability Visibility

The business can measure revenue and profitability, but management requires a clearer view of how revenue, profit, margins, discounts, returns, and other available factors interact.

**Impact:**

Difficulty identifying which high-revenue areas are also financially attractive.

---

### Gap 2 — Product-Level Performance Monitoring

Product and category performance varies, but further structured monitoring is required to identify products that require attention.

**Impact:**

Potentially underperforming products may not be identified quickly enough.

---

### Gap 3 — Return Rate Investigation

The Fashion category has a higher return rate than the overall business return rate.

**Impact:**

Potential additional operational workload and customer-experience concerns require investigation.

---

### Gap 4 — Customer Value Understanding

Revenue concentration exists across particular customer groups, but revenue alone does not fully describe customer value.

**Impact:**

The business may not have a complete view of which customer groups contribute most to profitable growth.

---

### Gap 5 — Insight-to-Action Gap

Power BI provides analytical insights, but those insights need to be converted into clearly documented business actions, requirements, owners, and measurable outcomes.

**Impact:**

Analytical findings may not automatically translate into business improvements.

---

## 4. Gap Prioritization

| Gap | Business Impact | Urgency | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| Profitability Visibility | High | High | High |
| Product Performance Monitoring | High | Medium | High |
| Return Rate Investigation | Medium/High | Medium | Medium |
| Customer Value Understanding | Medium/High | Medium | Medium |
| Insight-to-Action Gap | High | High | High |

Priority is based on the potential business impact and the need for further investigation.

---

## 5. Improvement Opportunities

Based on the identified gaps, the following opportunities should be considered:

1. Improve profitability monitoring.
2. Establish product and category performance monitoring.
3. Investigate high-return categories and products.
4. Improve customer-value analysis.
5. Monitor the relationship between discounts and profitability.
6. Strengthen operational KPI monitoring.
7. Convert analytical findings into documented business requirements.
8. Establish measurable management action plans.

---

## 7. Success Criteria

The future state should enable management to:

- Monitor revenue and profitability together.
- Identify high-performing and underperforming products.
- Detect unusual return-rate patterns.
- Understand important customer segments.
- Monitor operational performance.
- Investigate business performance gaps.
- Convert findings into actionable recommendations.
- Track improvement using measurable KPIs.

---

## 8. BA Conclusion

The primary gap identified is not simply a lack of data.

The business has analytical information available through SQL, Python, and Power BI.

The key gap is the transition from:

**Data → Insight → Root Cause → Requirement → Action → Measurement**

The Business Analysis phase will therefore focus on converting analytical findings into structured requirements, process improvements, user stories, and management actions.
