# Business Problem Definition

## 1. Project Overview

This project analyzes an e-commerce business using customer, product, sales, profitability, and operational data.

The objective is to understand business performance, identify areas of improvement, and provide data-driven recommendations that can support management decision-making.

The analysis combines data cleaning, SQL analysis, Python exploratory analysis, Power BI visualization, and Business Analysis techniques.

---

## 2. Business Problem

The e-commerce business generates revenue across multiple products, categories, customer segments, and other business dimensions. However, revenue performance alone does not provide a complete picture of business success.

Management needs to understand:

- Which products and categories contribute most to revenue and profit.
- Which products generate high revenue but comparatively lower profitability.
- Which customer segments contribute significantly to business performance.
- Which areas show potential operational or performance gaps.
- What factors may be contributing to differences in profitability.
- What actions can be taken to improve overall business performance.

The key business problem is to identify performance gaps and translate data-driven findings into actionable business recommendations.

---

## 3. Business Need

The business needs a structured analysis that can help management:

1. Understand current business performance.
2. Identify profitable and underperforming areas.
3. Understand customer and product behavior.
4. Identify potential causes of profitability gaps.
5. Prioritize improvement opportunities.
6. Make informed business decisions using measurable KPIs.

---

## 4. Business Objectives

The main objectives of the analysis are:

### Objective 1 — Revenue Analysis

Identify the major contributors to overall revenue across products, categories, customers, and other relevant dimensions.

### Objective 2 — Profitability Analysis

Identify products and categories with strong and weak profitability and investigate potential profitability gaps.

### Objective 3 — Customer Analysis

Understand customer purchasing behavior and identify customer groups that contribute significantly to revenue and profitability.

### Objective 4 — Operational Analysis

Analyze available operational metrics to identify potential performance issues and improvement opportunities.

### Objective 5 — Opportunity Identification

Identify areas where the business may be able to improve revenue, profitability, customer value, or operational efficiency.

### Objective 6 — Management Recommendations

Convert analytical findings into practical and measurable recommendations that management can evaluate and prioritize.

---

## 5. Key Business Questions

The analysis aims to answer the following questions:

1. Which products generate the highest revenue?
2. Which products generate the highest profit?
3. Which categories perform best in terms of revenue and profitability?
4. Are there products with high revenue but relatively low profit margins?
5. Which customer segments contribute most to business performance?
6. Which customer age groups generate significant revenue?
7. What patterns can be identified in customer purchasing behavior?
8. Are there operational areas that require attention?
9. What factors may be contributing to profitability differences?
10. Which business areas represent the greatest improvement opportunities?
11. What actions should management consider based on the findings?
12. How can the impact of these actions be measured?

- Customer analysis
- Customer segment analysis
- Age-group analysis
