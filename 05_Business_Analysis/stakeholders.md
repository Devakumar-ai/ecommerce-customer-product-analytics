# Stakeholder Analysis

## 1. Stakeholder Overview

Stakeholder analysis identifies the individuals or teams who are affected by the business problem, use the analysis outputs, or participate in business decision-making.

The following stakeholders are considered relevant to the e-commerce business analysis project.

---

## 2. Stakeholder Identification

| Stakeholder | Role | Primary Interest | Key Information Needed | Responsibility |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Senior Management | Strategic decision maker | Overall business performance | Revenue, Profit, Profit Margin, Growth, Key Risks | Strategic decisions |
| Finance Team | Financial management | Revenue and profitability | Revenue, Profit, Costs, Margins | Financial decisions |
| Sales Team | Sales performance | Product and customer sales | Sales trends, Products, Customers, Categories | Sales improvement |
| Marketing Team | Customer acquisition and retention | Customer behavior | Customer segments, Age Groups, Purchasing behavior | Marketing decisions |
| Operations Team | Order fulfillment and operational efficiency | Operational performance | Order metrics, Delivery performance, Process issues | Operational improvement |
| Product/Category Team | Product portfolio management | Product and category performance | Revenue, Profit, Margin, Product trends | Product decisions |
| Business Analyst | Analysis and decision support | Business problems and requirements | Business KPIs, Findings, Stakeholder needs | Analysis and recommendations |

---

## 3. Power–Interest Analysis

Stakeholders are categorized based on their level of influence over business decisions and their level of interest in the analysis.

### Manage Closely

- Senior Management
- Finance Team
- Sales Team
- Business Analyst

These stakeholders have significant influence or direct involvement in business decisions and require regular communication.

### Keep Satisfied

- Operations Team
- Product/Category Team

These stakeholders have important business responsibilities and should receive relevant insights and updates.

### Keep Informed

- Marketing Team

The marketing team requires relevant customer and purchasing insights to support business activities.

### Monitor

Other supporting stakeholders may be monitored depending on their involvement in specific business decisions.

---

## 4. Stakeholder Communication Needs

| Stakeholder | Communication Focus | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Senior Management | Key findings, risks, recommendations, KPIs | Periodic |
| Finance Team | Revenue, profit, margins, financial impact | Periodic |
| Sales Team | Product, category, and customer performance | Regular |
| Marketing Team | Customer segments and purchasing behavior | Regular |
| Operations Team | Operational findings and improvement opportunities | As required |
| Product/Category Team | Product profitability and performance | Regular |
| Business Analyst | Requirements, analysis, documentation, recommendations | Throughout project |

---

## 5. Stakeholder Expectations

### Senior Management
- Clear understanding of business performance.
- Identification of major business issues.
- Actionable recommendations.
- Measurable KPIs.

### Finance Team
- Accurate revenue and profitability analysis.
- Identification of profitability gaps.
- Financial impact of recommendations.

### Sales Team
- Understanding of high-performing and underperforming products.
- Customer and category performance insights.

### Marketing Team
- Understanding of customer segments.
- Identification of customer purchasing patterns.

### Operations Team
- Identification of operational performance gaps.
- Opportunities for process improvement.

### Product/Category Team
- Understanding of product and category profitability.
- Identification of products requiring further investigation.

### Business Analyst
- Clear business requirements.
- Stakeholder alignment.
- Data-driven findings.
- Actionable recommendations.

---

## 6. Stakeholder Management Approach

The Business Analyst will:

1. Identify stakeholder needs and expectations.
2. Gather relevant business requirements.
3. Communicate findings using appropriate KPIs and visualizations.
4. Validate business problems with relevant stakeholders.
5. Document requirements and decisions.
6. Translate analytical findings into actionable business recommendations.
