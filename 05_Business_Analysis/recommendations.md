# Business Recommendations & Management Action Plan

## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to translate the findings from the business analysis into actionable recommendations.

Recommendations are based on the observed business performance, identified gaps, and areas requiring further investigation.

The recommendations should be validated against additional business evidence before implementation.

---

## 2. Recommendation Framework

Recommendations are evaluated using the following factors:

- Business impact
- Urgency
- Feasibility
- Data availability
- Stakeholder ownership
- Measurability

---

## 3. Key Business Recommendations

### Recommendation 1 — Strengthen Profitability Monitoring

#### Business Issue

Revenue performance does not necessarily indicate proportional profitability across products and categories.

#### Recommendation

Establish a structured profitability monitoring approach that evaluates:

- Revenue
- Profit
- Profit Margin
- Discount
- Returns
- Product/category performance

#### Expected Outcome

Management can identify products and categories that generate strong revenue but require further profitability investigation.

#### KPI

- Total Profit
- Profit Margin
- Profit by Product
- Profit by Category

#### Owner

Finance / Product Management

---

### Recommendation 2 — Investigate High-Return Categories

#### Business Issue

The Fashion category has a higher return rate than the overall business return rate.

#### Recommendation

Conduct a product-level and customer-level investigation of Fashion returns to identify recurring patterns.

The investigation should consider:

- Product
- Customer segment
- Age group
- Order value
- Discount
- Return behavior

#### Expected Outcome

Potential drivers of higher return rates can be identified and addressed through appropriate business actions.

#### KPI

- Return Rate
- Return Count
- Category Return Rate
- Product Return Rate

#### Owner

Operations / Product Team

---

### Recommendation 3 — Improve Product Performance Monitoring

#### Business Issue

Product and category performance varies across the business.

#### Recommendation

Establish regular monitoring of product-level:

- Revenue
- Profit
- Profit Margin
- Units Sold
- Return Rate

#### Expected Outcome

Management can identify high-performing and underperforming products more efficiently.

#### KPI

- Product Revenue
- Product Profit
- Profit Margin
- Units Sold
- Return Rate

#### Owner

Product / Sales Team

---

### Recommendation 4 — Strengthen Customer Value Analysis

#### Business Issue

Revenue is concentrated among specific customer groups, but revenue alone does not fully describe customer value.

#### Recommendation

Analyze customer groups using multiple dimensions including:

- Revenue
- Order frequency
- Average Order Value
- Product preferences
- Profitability where available

#### Expected Outcome

The business can better understand high-value customer groups and their purchasing behavior.

#### KPI

- Revenue by Customer Segment
- Revenue by Age Group
- Customer Count
- Average Order Value
- Customer Profitability where available

#### Owner

Marketing / Sales Team

---

### Recommendation 5 — Monitor Discount Impact on Profitability

#### Business Issue

Discounting may influence profitability.

#### Recommendation

Analyze the relationship between discount levels and:

- Revenue
- Profit
- Profit Margin
- Product/category performance

#### Expected Outcome

Management can identify whether discounting is associated with meaningful changes in profitability.

#### KPI

- Discount Percentage
- Revenue
- Profit
- Profit Margin

#### Owner

Sales / Finance / Product Team

---

### Recommendation 6 — Strengthen Operational KPI Monitoring

#### Business Issue

Operational factors such as shipping, delivery, and returns may influence overall business performance.

#### Recommendation

Establish operational KPI monitoring and investigate relationships between operational performance and financial outcomes.

#### Expected Outcome

Potential operational inefficiencies can be identified and investigated.

#### KPI

- Order Count
- Delivery Metrics
- Shipping Metrics
- Return Rate
- Profitability Metrics

#### Owner

Operations Team

## 9. Final BA Recommendation

The analysis indicates that the business should move from primarily monitoring performance to systematically converting insights into measurable actions.

The recommended approach is to establish a continuous cycle:

**Monitor → Identify → Investigate → Act → Measure**

This approach allows management to use business data not only to understand what happened, but also to investigate why it happened and determine appropriate next steps.
