# As-Is Business Analysis

## 1. Purpose

The As-Is analysis describes the current state of the e-commerce business based on the available dataset and the completed SQL, Python, and Power BI analysis.

The objective is to understand current business performance, identify performance gaps, and establish a baseline for identifying improvement opportunities.

---

## 2. Current Business State

The e-commerce business operates across multiple products, categories, customer segments, age groups, regions, payment methods, and operational dimensions.

The analysis indicates that business performance varies across these dimensions. Revenue concentration, profitability, customer behavior, product performance, and return behavior are not uniform across the business.

The current-state analysis therefore focuses on identifying areas that require further investigation and potential improvement.

---

## 3. Revenue Performance

### Current Observations

- Revenue varies significantly across customer age groups.
- The 25–34 age group is currently the largest revenue-contributing age group.
- The South region contributes the highest revenue among the analyzed regions.
- Electronics is one of the strongest contributors to revenue and unit volume.
- Revenue performance is concentrated among specific customer and product segments.

### Business Implication

The business should understand which customer and product segments are responsible for the largest share of revenue and whether these segments also generate proportional profitability.

---

## 4. Customer Performance

### Current Observations

Customer revenue differs across age groups and customer segments.

The 25–34 age group contributes approximately ₹1.17M in revenue and represents an important customer group within the analyzed dataset.

Customer behavior should be evaluated not only by revenue but also by factors such as purchasing patterns, product preferences, and profitability where data is available.

### Business Implication

Customer segmentation can help the business identify high-value customer groups and understand where customer-focused strategies may have the greatest potential impact.

---

## 5. Product and Category Performance

### Current Observations

- Electronics demonstrates strong revenue and unit-volume performance.
- Product performance varies across categories.
- Revenue contribution does not necessarily indicate equivalent profitability.
- Category-level performance requires comparison of revenue, profit, and profit margin.

### Business Implication

Management should evaluate product and category performance using both revenue and profitability rather than relying on sales volume alone.

---

## 6. Returns Performance

### Current Observations

Return behavior varies across product categories.

The Fashion category has a return rate of approximately 8.28%, compared with an overall return rate of approximately 5.52%.

### Business Implication

The higher return rate in Fashion represents an area requiring further investigation.

Potential areas for investigation include:

- Product quality
- Product descriptions
- Size or fit-related issues
- Customer expectations
- Product-specific return patterns

No specific root cause should be assumed without additional evidence.

---

## 7. Regional Performance

### Current Observations

The South region currently contributes the highest revenue among the analyzed regions.

Revenue performance differs across regions, indicating that geographical analysis can provide useful insights into customer demand and business performance.

### Business Implication

Management can investigate whether regional differences are associated with customer demographics, product preferences, order volume, or other business factors.

---

## 8. Payment Behavior

### Current Observation

Credit Card is the most-used payment method in the analyzed dataset.

### Business Implication

Payment-method usage provides useful information about customer purchasing behavior and can support future analysis of payment preferences and transaction performance.

---

## 9. Operational Performance

The completed Power BI analysis also includes operational dimensions such as:

- Discounts
- Shipping
- Delivery
- Returns
- Profitability

These dimensions should be analyzed together with revenue and profit to determine whether operational factors may be contributing to business performance differences.

---

## 10. Current-State Performance Gaps

Based on the current analysis, the following areas require further investigation:

| Area | Current-State Observation | Business Concern |
|---|---|---|
| Profitability | Revenue and profitability may not move proportionally across products/categories | High sales may not always result in strong profit |
| Product Performance | Performance varies across categories | Some products/categories may require attention |
| Returns | Fashion return rate is higher than overall return rate | Potential operational/customer-experience issue |
| Customer Segments | Revenue is concentrated among certain age groups | Opportunity to understand high-value customers |
| Regional Performance | South contributes the highest revenue | Regional performance differences require investigation |
| Discounts | Discounting can affect profitability | Need to understand relationship between discount and profit |
| Operations | Delivery, shipping, and returns affect overall performance | Potential efficiency and cost opportunities |

---

## 11. As-Is Business Questions

The following questions require deeper analysis:

1. Which products generate high revenue but relatively low profit?
2. Which categories have the strongest and weakest profit margins?
3. What is the relationship between discounts and profitability?
4. Why is the Fashion return rate higher than the overall return rate?
5. Which customer segments generate the highest profit, not just revenue?
6. What factors contribute to regional revenue differences?
7. Are shipping and delivery factors affecting profitability?
8. Which business areas should management investigate first?
9. Which performance gaps can be addressed through process improvements?
10. Which KPIs should be monitored after improvements are implemented?

---

## 12. Current-State Summary

The current-state analysis shows that the e-commerce business has multiple dimensions that require simultaneous evaluation.

Revenue performance is concentrated among particular customer, product, and regional segments, while profitability, returns, discounts, shipping, and delivery provide additional dimensions for understanding business performance.

The key BA requirement at this stage is to move from identifying performance differences to understanding their potential causes and business impact.

The next stage is therefore Root Cause Analysis.

---

The following areas will be investigated using structured root-cause analysis:

- High-revenue / low-profit products
