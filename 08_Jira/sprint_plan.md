# Agile Sprint Plan

## 1. Project Overview

### Project Name

E-Commerce Customer & Product Analytics

### Purpose

This Sprint Plan defines how the E-Commerce Customer & Product Analytics solution will be delivered incrementally using an Agile approach.

The project is divided into manageable sprints, with each sprint producing a usable and validated business outcome.

---

## 2. Agile Delivery Approach

The project will follow an iterative Agile delivery model.

Each sprint will include:

1. Sprint Planning
2. Requirement Review
3. Development / Analysis
4. Testing and Validation
5. Business Review
6. Sprint Review
7. Retrospective

---

## 3. Sprint Structure

| Sprint | Duration | Primary Objective | Key Deliverables |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sprint 1 | 1 Week | Data Foundation | Data validation, cleaning, data model |
| Sprint 2 | 1 Week | Revenue & Sales Analytics | Revenue dashboard, sales KPIs |
| Sprint 3 | 1 Week | Customer & Product Analytics | Customer and product analysis |
| Sprint 4 | 1 Week | Profitability & Operations | Profitability matrix, operational analysis |
| Sprint 5 | 1 Week | Management Insights | Recommendations, action plan, executive dashboard |
| Sprint 6 | 1 Week | Validation & Final Delivery | Testing, documentation, final dashboard |

---

## 4. Sprint 1 – Data Foundation

### Objective

Prepare a reliable and analysis-ready e-commerce dataset.

### User Stories

- US-01
- US-02
- US-03

### Tasks

- Import source dataset
- Perform data profiling
- Identify missing values
- Identify duplicate records
- Validate data types
- Standardize categorical values
- Clean inconsistent records
- Create calculated fields
- Prepare analytical data model

### Acceptance Criteria

- Dataset contains no unintended duplicate records
- Required fields are available
- Data types are correctly defined
- Missing values are handled according to defined rules
- Dataset is ready for dashboard development

### Sprint Outcome

Validated and analysis-ready dataset.

---

## 5. Sprint 2 – Revenue & Sales Analytics

### Objective

Provide visibility into overall revenue and sales performance.

### Key Deliverables

- Revenue KPI
- Sales trend analysis
- Revenue by category
- Revenue by region
- Revenue by customer segment

### Acceptance Criteria

- Revenue KPIs calculate correctly
- Users can filter results by analysis period
- Revenue trends are visible
- Category and regional performance can be compared

### Sprint Outcome

Revenue and sales performance dashboard.

---

## 6. Sprint 3 – Customer & Product Analytics

### Objective

Understand customer behavior and product performance.

### Key Deliverables

- Customer analysis
- Customer age-group analysis
- Product performance analysis
- Top and bottom performing products
- Customer segmentation

### Acceptance Criteria

- Customer metrics are accurately calculated
- Product performance can be compared
- Users can identify high-value customer segments
- Top and bottom products are clearly visible

### Sprint Outcome

Customer and product analytics dashboard.

---

## 7. Sprint 4 – Profitability & Operational Analysis

### Objective

Identify profitability drivers and operational performance gaps.

### Key Deliverables

- Profitability analysis
- Profit margin analysis
- Profitability Matrix
- Discount analysis
- Return analysis
- Operational KPI analysis

### Acceptance Criteria

- Profitability metrics are correctly calculated
- Products can be classified by revenue and profitability
- High-revenue / low-profit products can be identified
- Discount and return patterns can be analyzed

### Sprint Outcome

Profitability and operational analysis dashboard.

---

## 8. Sprint 5 – Management Insights

### Objective

Convert analytical findings into actionable business recommendations.

### Key Deliverables

- Root-cause analysis
- Key business findings
- Management recommendations
- Management Action Plan
- Executive dashboard

### Acceptance Criteria

- Major performance gaps are documented
- Findings are supported by dashboard evidence
- Recommendations are linked to identified problems
- Actions have defined owners and priorities
- Expected business impact is documented

### Sprint Outcome

Management decision-support dashboard and action plan.

---

## 9. Sprint 6 – Validation & Final Delivery

### Objective

Validate the complete analytics solution and prepare it for business handover.

### Activities

- Validate KPIs
- Test dashboard filters
- Validate calculations
- Review user stories
- Validate acceptance criteria
- Perform stakeholder review
- Fix identified issues
- Finalize documentation
- Prepare GitHub repository

### Acceptance Criteria

- All critical user stories meet acceptance criteria
- Dashboard calculations are validated
- Filters work correctly
- Documentation is complete
- Final solution is ready for presentation

### Sprint Outcome

Validated and documented E-Commerce Customer & Product Analytics solution.

---

## 10. Definition of Done

A backlog item is considered complete when:

- Requirement is clearly understood
- Development / analysis is completed
- Data calculations are validated
- Acceptance criteria are satisfied
- Business logic is documented
- Testing is completed
- Stakeholder review is completed where required
- No critical defects remain
- Relevant documentation is updated

---

## 11. Sprint Review

At the end of each sprint, the completed work will be reviewed against:

- Business requirements
- Functional requirements
- User stories
- Acceptance criteria
- Expected business outcomes

Feedback identified during the review will be converted into backlog items where required.

---

## 12. Sprint Retrospective

The team will review:

- What went well
- What did not go well
- What should be improved
- Actions for the next sprint
